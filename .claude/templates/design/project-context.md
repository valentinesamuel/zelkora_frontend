# Project Context

The business source of truth. Authored at stage 0, read by every stage. Every
specialist reconstructs its understanding of the product from this file, so
vagueness here degrades every stage downstream.

Replace every `{...}` placeholder. Delete nothing.

## Product

{What this product is, in two sentences, without marketing language.}

## Users

{Who uses it. How often — daily for hours, weekly, monthly? How expert are they in
the domain, and in software generally? This single answer drives density,
keyboard-first design and how much guidance the interface carries.}

## Primary job

{The single job of the main surface. One sentence.}

## Deployment context

{Internal tool, customer-facing product, or both. Who sees it, and what would
embarrass you.}

## Scale

{Record counts, concurrent users, data growth. Real numbers where known, honest
estimates where not, marked as estimates.}

## Domain vocabulary

{The terms users actually use, with meanings. Wrong domain language reads as
outsider work faster than any visual mistake.}

## Technology

{Deviations from `.claude/knowledge/stack.md`, and why. Routing library choice.
Existing codebase, if any, and its state.}

## Brand and visual constraints

{Existing brand, mandated fonts or colours, accessibility requirements beyond WCAG
AA, or an explicit statement that the art direction is open.}

## Out of scope

{What this product deliberately does not do.}

## Non-negotiables

{Compliance, security, regulatory, or organisational constraints that override
design preference.}
